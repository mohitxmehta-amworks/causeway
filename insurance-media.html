Media Liability Insurance

Coverage for the work you publish.

Defamation, copyright, invasion of privacy, and the other risks that come with putting work into the world. If you publish, report, film, or post, media liability is the coverage most independent creators go without.

Start here. Share a few basics and Causeway builds your file, then sits with you to complete the underwriting questions and bring back quotes, matched to what you actually do.

What it covers

Protection built for publishing.

An LLC protects your business. Media liability protects you when the work itself draws a claim, the kind a general business policy leaves out.

Causeway helps you get the right coverage in place, sized to your reach, your format, and the kind of content you make.

  • Defamation, libel & slander
  • Copyright & trademark infringement
  • Invasion of privacy & right of publicity
  • Breach of confidence & misappropriation of ideas
  • Defense costs, not just settlements
