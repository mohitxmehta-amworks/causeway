Causeway Values

What we hold ourselves to.

Causeway’s mission is to improve public discourse by making it easier to create and distribute high quality, free expression. We believe a flourishing community of independent media is central to that aim.

With that goal in mind, we aspire to hold ourselves and all partners or members of Causeway to the following values.

  1. A commitment to reality

    Not all free expression seeks to document reality. For that which does, there are many methods to produce something of value. Regardless of format or tactic, we expect all members of our community to use reasonable, data-informed, factual assessments when making direct representations about the world. It is unacceptable to knowingly or negligently report inaccurate information.

  2. A commitment to respecting democratic institutions

    We expect all members of our community to accept and respect the importance of a functioning democracy, including the importance of the rule of law. Actions or inducements to break the law or in some other way undermine the need for procedural justice is unacceptable.

  3. A commitment to prosocial outcomes

    We believe that the more authority one has, the more obligation one has to support the broader community. We aspire to take all appropriate care not to endanger or exploit the audiences or partners with whom we engage. We expect members of our community to act in the same way.

These values incorporate no partisan or ideological requirement; we encourage anyone who abides by these values to participate.
