Healthcare

Group health, for people who work alone.

Independent creators get priced as a group of one. Causeway members get into a large-group plan instead, by being employed part-time through Causeway.

That employment has to be real, which means income has to run through it. The floor is a $25,000 annual contract, and it can arrive in as many as four quarterly payments. The section below explains exactly how that works before you fill anything out.

Members only. Healthcare support is available to Causeway members. If you haven't joined yet, fill out the membership form first and we'll pick this up right after.

How it works

Coverage comes with employment.

The reason a solo creator pays individual-market rates is that there is no employer in the picture. Causeway becomes one. You are employed part-time by Causeway through Justworks, our PEO, and that is what puts you inside a large-group plan instead of a pool of one.

For that to hold up, the employment has to be genuine. You have to be paid, and the money has to come from somewhere real. So we either sign a contract on your behalf with one of your clients, or you route an existing income source through Causeway. Either way it has to total at least $25,000 a year.

The money stays yours. It covers your premiums, your payroll taxes, and the Justworks fee. Everything left over is paid to you as wages.

  • Large-group medical, with dental and vision options
  • Coverage for spouses, partners, and children
  • Payroll, withholding, and filings handled through Justworks
  • W-2 wages on record instead of loose 1099 income

The $25,000 minimum

Where the money comes from.

This is the part people have the most questions about, so here it is plainly. Nothing below is a fee you pay Causeway. It is your own income, running through a structure that makes group coverage possible.

1

A contract or an income source

Either Causeway signs a contract on your behalf with one of your clients, or you route income you already have through Causeway. A brand deal, a retainer, a production budget, a grant. It has to be real work with a real payer.

2

$25,000 a year, in up to four payments

Twenty-five thousand is a hard floor, not a target. It can arrive as one payment or split into as many as four quarterly tranches. Below that number the employment doesn't hold up, and we would rather say so early than unwind it later.

3

What comes out of it

  • Your health premiums
  • Payroll taxes
  • $110 a month for Justworks
  • Everything else is paid to you as wages, net of withholding.

Causeway does not take a margin on the contract. The money that comes in is yours. It passes through payroll, covers the three items above, and the rest lands in your account as W-2 wages. The reason the contract has to exist at all is that group coverage runs through employment, and employment runs through pay.

This describes the structure as we are building it, and the underlying agreements are being finalized with counsel. Specific terms, eligibility, and timing may change. None of this is tax, legal, or benefits advice, and you should talk to your own accountant about how running income through payroll affects your situation.
