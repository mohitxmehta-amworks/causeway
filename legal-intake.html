How it works

You don't have to categorize it.

Legal questions rarely arrive sorted. Something that looks like a template problem turns out to need advice, and something that feels urgent turns out to be routine. Sorting it is our job, not yours.

Describe the situation in whatever shape it's in. We'll work out what's actually being asked and where it should go.

  • Contracts and negotiation
  • Media liability and publishing risk
  • Privacy and data questions
  • Entity formation and business structure
  • Nonprofit compliance
  • Templates for routine paperwork
