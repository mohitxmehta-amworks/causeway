Legal

Start by figuring out what you need.

Most creators are not sure whether the thing in front of them is a legal problem, a paperwork problem, or nothing at all. That is the part we help with.

Causeway is not a law firm. We do not give legal advice. We help you work out what kind of help you need, or whether you need any, and connect you with independent attorneys who work with creators.

How it works

Tell us what is going on.

It does not need to be a clean question. Things rarely arrive that way. Sometimes it is a contract you were sent, sometimes it is a message you are not sure how to read, sometimes it is a nagging sense that something should have been set up a while ago.

Members come to us about:

  • Contracts and negotiation
  • Media liability and publishing risk
  • Privacy and data questions
  • Entity formation and business structure
  • Nonprofit compliance
  • Templates for routine paperwork

What happens next

From a rough question to a clear one.

  • 1You describe the situation. A few sentences is plenty.
  • 2We read it and come back with what we think the actual question is.
  • 3If it calls for a lawyer, we introduce you to one. If it does not, we say so.
  • 4You take it from there with them directly.

Attorneys we introduce you to are independent and separately responsible for their own advice and their own fees.

Where the line sits

What we do not do.

Worth being plain about, because it shapes what you should expect from us.

  • We do not give legal advice or act as your attorney.
  • We do not draft, review, or negotiate your documents.
  • We do not represent you or take on responsibility for how a matter turns out.
  • We do not decide for you whether to engage counsel, and we do not sit between you and the attorney once you do.

Causeway is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal services. Nothing on this page creates an attorney-client relationship. This page is general information only.

Not sure where to start?

That is usually the right time to ask. Members get a short intake form and we take it from there.
